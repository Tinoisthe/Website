# Public site (Home + Pricing + About + Roadmap + FAQ + Contact + Legal)

Mode: Persuade (Home, Pricing, About, Contact); Read (Roadmap, FAQ, Legal) in the same world.
Audience: developers and self-hosters first; web-hosting buyers second. Action: pick a plan and click through to billing. Proof: real specs, AUD prices, stock status, hardware partners, real team, Discord. No invented testimonials.
Constraints: values static and copied unchanged from the current site; owner copy questions listed in README.

History: a first build in a new "Harbour Light Festival" world was rejected by the user (preferred the original look; sections not distinguishable; not attractive to paying customers). Replaced by a refinement of the incumbent identity.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The current Neon Next Generation look, done properly. Same brand a returning customer recognises, now with clear section rhythm, scannable plan cards and readable type. Refuses both the rejected festival world and a generic white SaaS template.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black violet ground #050208 with surfaces #0d0a14 / #130f1d. Brand violet #9b5de5, deep #7b2ff2, glow #b47aff; secondary green #34d399; amber #fbbf24 for out-of-stock/coming-soon. Inter throughout (600/700 tight-tracked headings, the second heading line in the violet→green brand gradient as on the incumbent). Rounded 16–20px glass cards with hairline violet borders, pill buttons (gradient primary, ghost secondary), soft radial violet/green glow washes that change per section so every section has its own atmosphere.

STORY: Visitor sees the brand, a real panel and a deploy terminal, understands it is Australian hosting at hyperscaler-beating prices, compares plan cards by their spec strip, trusts the infrastructure and real team, then deploys or joins Discord.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 6/12: headline "Next-Gen / Hosting (gradient) / Infrastructure", lede, primary gradient pill "Deploy VPS · from AU$5.94/mo", ghost "Deploy VPC", inline facts row. Right 6/12: hosting-panel window mock (browser chrome hosting.neonnextgeneration.com, sidebar, servers card) with three metric chips beneath and the deploy terminal overlapping its lower edge.

FORM: Refinement of the incumbent world (no concept roll; the user pinned the existing identity). Signature interaction: product switcher (VPS / VPC / Web) that crossfades the card grid, with the popular card lifted in a violet glow ring; cards lift and brighten their border on hover.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
